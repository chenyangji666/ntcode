\title[Enhancing Spatial Transcriptomics Resolution]{Computational Methods for Enhancing Spatial Transcriptomics Resolution to the Single-Cell Level: A Systematic Review of Deconvolution, Mapping, and Reconstruction Approaches}

\abstract{Sequencing-based spatial transcriptomics platforms such as Visium capture transcriptome-wide expression but at spot-level resolution, where each measurement aggregates signals from multiple cells. Recovering single-cell information from these mixed observations is necessary for downstream analyses including cell--cell communication inference, niche characterisation, and biomarker discovery. Here we provide the first systematic review covering the complete computational pipeline from spots to cells, organised into four progressive stages: (I) spot-level deconvolution to estimate cell-type proportions, (II) single-cell mapping to assign reference cells to spatial coordinates, (III) pseudo single-cell reconstruction to synthesise per-cell expression profiles at sub-spot resolution, and (IV) multi-modal and image-guided enhancement using histology or paired modalities. We survey more than 25 representative methods published between 2020 and 2026, propose a two-axis taxonomy based on output resolution and information source, and provide a practical decision tree for method selection. We delineate what pseudo single-cell reconstruction can and cannot reliably infer, identify five open challenges, and discuss future directions including foundation models for spatial omics and the implications of emerging sub-cellular platforms.}

\keywords{spatial transcriptomics, cell-type deconvolution, single-cell mapping, pseudo single-cell reconstruction, Visium, multi-modal integration}
